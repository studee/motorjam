MotorJam Racer – Privacy Policy

Effective October 20, 2026

MotorJam Racer is a game for Android. This policy explains what happens to your information when you play it.

What we collect

Nothing. The game does not collect, store on a server, or sell any personal information. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no in-app purchases. Everything you do in single-player stays on your phone, and single-player doesn't use the internet at all.

Online races

If you choose ONLINE to race another player, the game connects to the internet, and only then:

  • Matchmaking. Your phone connects to our matchmaking service (hosted on Cloudflare) to be paired with another rider, either by quick match or with a room code. Like any internet connection, this reveals your IP address to the service. It is used only to pair you (riders on the same network are paired first), to place the connection near you, and to protect the service from abuse (rate limits). It is kept in memory only while you are being matched and is not stored or logged by us. As our hosting provider, Cloudflare processes connection data under its own privacy policy.
  • The race. Once paired, your phone connects directly to the other rider's phone, so each phone sees the other's IP address. If a direct connection isn't possible, the race goes through our relay, also on Cloudflare, which only passes the race messages between the two phones and keeps nothing. What's exchanged: the bike you ride, its position and speed during the race, and your finish time. No names, no chat, no photos, no accounts.
  • Online results are not saved anywhere, not even in your career.

What stays on your device

Your game progress and settings (stars, coins, unlocked roads and bikes, best times, and options such as the music volume) are saved in the app's private storage on your phone. They never leave the device. Uninstalling the game deletes them.

Permissions

The game uses vibration for haptic feedback, and may read the phone's motion sensors if you choose tilt steering. Sensor readings are used only while you play and are not stored or sent anywhere. It uses the internet only for online races (above).

Children

The game does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.
