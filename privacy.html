MotorJam Racer – Privacy Policy

Effective October 11, 2026

MotorJam Racer is a game for Android. This policy explains what happens to your information when you play it.

What we collect

Nothing. The game does not collect, store on a server, or share any personal information. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no in-app purchases, and it does not use the internet at all (it does not request the internet permission).

What stays on your device

Your game progress and settings (stars, coins, unlocked roads and bikes, best times, and options such as the music volume) are saved in the app's private storage on your phone. They never leave the device. Uninstalling the game deletes them.

Permissions

The game uses vibration for haptic feedback, and may read the phone's motion sensors if you choose tilt steering. Sensor readings are used only while you play and are not stored or sent anywhere.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
